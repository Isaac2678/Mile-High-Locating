What working with us looks like

You talk to the locator

We're owner-operated. The person who answers the phone is the person who shows up and marks your site — no dispatch queue, no relaying details through an office.

Scheduling that fits a job

Most Front Range jobs are scheduled within 1–3 business days. We're available Monday through Saturday. For anything urgent, call — we'll do our best to fit you in.

Marks your crew can use

Lines are marked on the surface in standard APWA colors, and we walk your foreman or super through what we found. Need it for the project file? Ask for a written locate report with GPS-tagged photos.

How to request a locate

Call or send the details online. Here's what helps us quote and schedule quickly.

Rate: $150/hr, 2-hour minimum. Travel fee may apply outside the Denver metro.

What to send us

  • Jobsite address — or the nearest cross streets for new construction
  • Where you're digging — the area or route of the work
  • Type of work and roughly how deep you're going
  • What you're worried about, if you know — gas, power to an outbuilding, lighting
  • When you need it marked and when you plan to dig
  • On-site contact and any gate or access details

Then what happens

  1. We confirm scope, timing and price with you.
  2. We sweep the work area and mark the private lines we find.
  3. We walk you through the marks and anything we couldn't trace.

Get your 811 ticket in too. A private locate covers the private side of the site, not the member utilities — you still need 811 before you dig.
