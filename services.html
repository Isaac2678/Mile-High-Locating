General Private Line Locating

Not sure what's buried on a property? We sweep the work area and mark every private utility we can detect before excavation starts. This is the right call for contractors starting on an unfamiliar site, commercial properties with several buildings, and any property where the owner doesn't know what's been installed over the years.

What's included

  • Electromagnetic sweep of your work area or the full site
  • Surface marking with standard APWA utility color codes
  • A walkthrough of what we found — and anything we couldn't trace
  • Written locate report available on request

Common projects

  • Pre-construction and site prep
  • Trenching and new service runs
  • Fencing and landscaping
  • Footings, additions and outbuildings

Private Gas Line Locating

Private gas lines — the run from the meter to the house, a shop, a pool heater or a fire pit, and propane lines from the tank — aren't covered by 811. A gas strike is one of the most dangerous and expensive hits on any job. We locate and mark private gas lines before anyone digs near them.

What's included

  • Gas lines from the meter to the point of use
  • Propane supply lines from the tank
  • Lines near structures where excavation is planned
  • Documentation that supports your due diligence under C.R.S. 9-1.5

Secondary Electric Locating

Secondary electric lines run from the meter or transformer to outbuildings, detached garages, shops, sub-panels, site lighting and outdoor equipment. They're privately owned and 811 won't mark them. We trace and mark them so your crew knows exactly where they run.

What's included

  • Lines from the meter or transformer to secondary structures
  • Feeds to outbuildings, garages, shops and outdoor panels
  • Parking lot, sign and site lighting circuits
  • Common on rural properties, farms and commercial sites with multiple buildings

What we can locate

We use professional-grade electromagnetic (EM) locating equipment from Radiodetection and Vivax-Metrotech. EM locating finds lines that can carry a signal:

  • Metallic pipe, including steel gas lines and copper water lines
  • Electric cable — secondary power, lighting and equipment feeds
  • Communication cable with a metallic conductor
  • Plastic pipe that has tracer wire installed

What can limit a locate

Plastic or clay pipe without tracer wire, and lines with a break in the conductor, may not show up on EM equipment. If there's something we can't trace, we'll tell you on site so you know where to dig with extra care.
