Why you still need a private locate after calling 811

811 stops at the meter

When you call 811, member utilities mark the lines they own — typically ending at the meter or property line. Gas lines from the meter to the house, power run out to a detached garage or shop, parking lot lighting and other privately installed lines aren't theirs to mark. On a lot of sites, those are the lines that get hit.

What 811 does and doesn't mark

A strike stops the job

A hit line can mean a shutdown, repair bills, liability exposure and someone getting hurt. A private locate takes hours. Cleaning up after a strike can take weeks.

Colorado law & due diligence

Colorado's Underground Damage Prevention Law (C.R.S. 9-1.5) requires excavators to take reasonable precautions. A private locate is a documented step showing you did.

How a private locate works

One call, one locator, marks on the ground before you dig.

  1. Send us the jobsite

    Call or request a locate online with the address, what you're digging and when you need to start.

  2. We schedule

    Most Front Range jobs are scheduled within 1–3 business days. If it's urgent, call — we'll do our best to fit you in.

  3. We sweep and mark

    We trace the private lines in your work area and mark them on the surface using standard APWA utility color codes.

  4. We walk you through it

    Before we leave, we go over what we found and anything we couldn't trace. Written locate reports are available on request.
